何其锜Qqi He

我是音乐音频算法工程师，现任职于网易云音乐，硕士毕业于复旦大学音频音乐实验室（FD-LAMT）。研究专注于多模态音乐理解、音乐生成与音乐信息检索领域。I am an AI music researcher @ NetEase Cloud Music. I hold a master’s degree from the Audio and Music Technology Lab at Fudan University (FD-LAMT). My research focuses on multimodal music understanding, music generation, and music information retrieval.
